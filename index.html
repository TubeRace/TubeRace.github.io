The Rules

From the selected starting station and ending at or near the selected finishing station, each participant must visit 5 randomly selected train stations in the shortest time possible. Each participant will be visiting the same stations. The order is up to them.

  • A participant must not use their (or any other) phone for directions or transport information. They must only use their wits, knowledge, and a paper tube map.
  • Transportation is limited to rail of any type and foot passage. Participants may not use a bicycle (push, e-, or motorised), taxi, bus, e-scooter, or cable car, nor any other type of automobile. (You may choose to run the entire thing if you want to).
  • Visiting a required station is achieved by tapping out of the station, leaving the station building, and taking a selfie of yourself with a sign stating the name of the station.
  • The winner is the first person to reach the designated finish line with selfies of themself outside all 5 generated stations.
